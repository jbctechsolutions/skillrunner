## 3. Willingness-to-Pay Evidence

### 3.1 Fractional CTO Buyer Psychology

- **Hourly rate:** $200-500/hr. A $49/month tool that saves 1 hour/month pays for itself 4-10x over.
- **Monthly engagement budgets:** $3,000-$15,000/month. Tools are a rounding error at <$100/month.
- **Decision pattern:** Fractional CTOs are technical, cost-conscious, and prefer tools they control. Open-source with optional paid tiers is the optimal model.
- **Quote:** "In 2025-2026, the fractional model has become the default for Series A and bootstrapped SaaS companies." -- [UX Continuum](https://uxcontinuum.com/blog/startup-cto/fractional-cto-startups)

### 3.2 Developer Tool Pricing Psychology

Key principles from [Heavybit](https://www.heavybit.com/library/article/pricing-developer-tools) and [Monetizely](https://www.getmonetizely.com/articles/how-to-price-developer-tools-technical-feature-gating-and-quality-based-tiers-for-saas-success):

1. **Developers resist opaque pricing.** Clear, transparent tiers are mandatory.
2. **Freemium adoption path:** Individual discovers tool -> uses free tier on side project -> brings to work -> team adoption -> enterprise procurement.
3. **Every paid feature is evaluated against:** "Can I get this from an open-source alternative?"
4. **Generous free tiers drive adoption.** If free users can't accomplish meaningful work, they never develop tool habits.
5. **Companies that iterate on pricing quarterly grow 30% faster** than those adjusting annually.
6. **Feature gating, not capability gating:** Free users get full core functionality; paid users get scale, collaboration, and enterprise features.

### 3.3 Church/Non-Profit Willingness to Pay

- Small nonprofits spend 13.2% of budget on IT, but absolute budgets are small.
- SaaS subscription model preferred (81.64% of nonprofit software revenue).
- Cloud deployment preferred (78.16%).
- **Sweet spot:** $29-79/month for a tool that replaces 2-3 other subscriptions or saves significant volunteer/staff time.
- Churches are price-sensitive but value-responsive -- they will pay for tools that demonstrably save staff time or enable ministry outcomes they couldn't achieve otherwise.

## 5. Recommended Pricing Strategy for Skillrunner

### 5.1 Pricing Model: Open-Core + Consultant Multiplier

Skillrunner should follow the n8n/Dify model: free open-source core with paid tiers for scale and collaboration.

### 5.2 Proposed Tier Structure

| Tier | Price | Target | Key Features |
|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| **Community** | Free forever | Individual fractional CTOs, hobbyists | Full CLI, unlimited local workflows, YAML authoring, single-user, self-hosted only |
| **Pro** | $49/month | Solo fractional CTOs with 2-5 clients | Multi-client workspace management, Open WebUI integration templates, priority support, cloud-hosted option, 10 client deployments |
| **Team** | $149/month | Fractional CTO firms (2-5 consultants) | Team collaboration, shared workflow library, client analytics dashboard, 50 client deployments, white-label Open WebUI themes |
| **Enterprise** | $499/month (or custom) | MSPs, larger consultancies | Unlimited deployments, SSO/SAML, SLA support, custom integrations, dedicated onboarding |

### 5.3 Rationale

1. **$49/month Pro tier** is the sweet spot:
   - Below the "approval required" threshold for most fractional CTOs ($3K-15K/month engagement budgets).
   - Saves at least 1 hour/month at $200-500/hr rates = 4-10x ROI.
   - Competitive with n8n Pro ($60/mo) and below Dify Pro ($59/mo).
   - Within church/non-profit budget comfort zone ($29-79/mo).

2. **Free Community tier** is essential:
   - Developer tools require a generous free tier for adoption (per pricing psychology research).
   - Competes with n8n Community and Dify Sandbox.
   - Drives the adoption funnel: discover -> use free -> bring to client -> upgrade.

3. **$149/month Team tier** captures the "firm" buyer:
   - Fractional CTO firms are growing rapidly (market doubled 2022-2024).
   - Per-workspace, not per-user, avoids the PSA trap of $50/user/month scaling.

4. **$499/month Enterprise** leaves room for MSPs and larger orgs without underpricing.

### 5.4 Revenue Model Projections (Conservative)

| Scenario | Users | Mix | Monthly Revenue | ARR |
|----------|-------|-----|-----------------|-----|
| Year 1 (launch) | 200 free, 30 Pro, 5 Team, 1 Enterprise | 85/13/2/<1% | $2,719 | $32,628 |
| Year 2 (traction) | 1,000 free, 150 Pro, 25 Team, 5 Enterprise | 85/13/2/<1% | $12,575 | $150,900 |
| Year 3 (growth) | 5,000 free, 500 Pro, 100 Team, 20 Enterprise | 89/9/2/<1% | $49,350 | $592,200 |

### 5.5 Go-to-Market Pricing Tactics

1. **Launch with generous free tier** -- no time limits, no feature crippling. Gate on client deployment count and collaboration.
2. **Offer annual billing at 20% discount** ($470/yr for Pro instead of $588) -- nonprofits budget annually.
3. **Church/non-profit discount:** 25% off all paid tiers. Builds goodwill, drives word-of-mouth in tight-knit church tech communities.
4. **"First 100 Pro users" lifetime pricing** at $29/month -- creates urgency, builds early community, locks in evangelists.
5. **Iterate pricing quarterly** -- companies that do this grow 30% faster (per Monetizely research).
